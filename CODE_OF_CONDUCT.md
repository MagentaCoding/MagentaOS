## 1. Purpose of this document

This Code of Conduct sets out the binding rules for behavior within the official MagentaOS GitHub repository. It applies to all forms of interaction, including discussions, issues, pull requests, comments, commits, and other contributions.

The goal is to ensure a professional, respectful, and topic-related working environment that focuses exclusively on the development and documentation of MagentaOS.

## 2. Community Rules

### 2.1 Rules for discussions, comments, and posts

The following basic rules apply to all comments and content in the repository:

- No NSFW content or other inappropriate content
- No political content or political opinions
- No insults, disparagement, or personal attacks
- No deliberately provocative, disrespectful, or disruptive behavior
- No content that aims to verbally attack or disparage other people

MagentaOS is a **politically neutral project**. Political discussions, regardless of country, ideology, or orientation, have no place in the project.

### 2.2 Issues and Pull Requests

The rules of conduct for comments in issues and pull requests are identical to the rules for discussions and comments in the repository.

In addition:

- Posts must be factual, technical, and project-related
- Off-topic discussions may be closed or removed
- Maintainers reserve the right to moderate content without warning

## 3. Rules for code and content in the project

MagentaOS is and will remain a **content-neutral operating system**. The code, documentation, and all official content of the project must not contain any opinions or messages that lie outside the technical context of MagentaOS.

These include in particular:

- Political opinions or ideologies
- Activist or ideological content
- Social, religious, or identity-related statements

The project serves exclusively technical, functional, and documentary purposes.

This neutrality does **not constitute disregard for personal beliefs**. The personal opinions or identities of individual contributors are respected, but are **not part of the project** and must not be incorporated into code, documentation, or official content.

## 4. Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in the following measures, depending on their severity:

- Removal or editing of content
- Closing of issues or pull requests
- Warnings
- Temporary or permanent exclusion from the repository

The decision on measures to be taken lies with the MagentaOS team or the project owner.
